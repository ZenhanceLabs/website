# Zenhance Labs — design direction

This site is an original adaptation inspired by the Vercel entry in [VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md). It keeps the useful principles—black-and-white precision, generous whitespace, mono technical labels, hairline borders, and one atmospheric mesh gradient—without copying a brand lockup or page composition.

## Rules

- Use near-white `#fafafa` as the canvas, `#171717` as ink, and `#4d4d4d` for supporting copy.
- Keep display type sentence-case, lightly weighted, and tightly tracked. Japanese text uses `Noto Sans JP`; Latin text uses `Inter`.
- Use `IBM Plex Mono` only for eyebrows, status labels, and small metadata.
- Keep cards crisp: 1px hairline borders, 8–16px corners, and stacked soft shadows instead of floating glass panels.
- Reserve cyan / blue / violet / pink / amber for one large atmospheric gradient or meaningful product accent.
- Prefer direct, specific copy over generic startup language. Every interaction should tell the visitor where a product leads.
- Motion is limited to small reveal transitions and directional hover cues, with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Page rhythm

1. Brand and proposition
2. Two product cards with direct links
3. Three working principles
4. One clear contact route
